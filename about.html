Company Overview

Founded in 2013 and located in Nanjing, Jiangsu, China, Nanjing Suke Photoelectric Technology Co., Ltd. operates under the overseas brand SUKE Acrylic. The company specializes in the manufacturing of extruded PMMA sheets and also produces PC and PS plastic sheets for industrial and optical applications. Our main product range includes PMMA diffusion sheets, light guide sheets, and panel sheets, widely used in LED backlight modules, LED lighting systems, ultra-thin light boxes, and advertising display applications. The company is equipped with professional PMMA extrusion production lines, with a current monthly capacity of approximately 240 tons and an annual output exceeding 5,000 tons. Raw materials from optical-grade suppliers such as Sumitomo and Mitsubishi are selected to ensure stable thickness control and consistent optical performance.

Production follows a standardized quality management system, supported by batch control mechanisms to maintain consistency and reliability for bulk orders. With an experienced production team and structured export processes, we support long-term cooperation in technical specification confirmation, mass production, and order management.

Our products are currently exported to North America, Europe, and Japan, with established experience in full-container shipments and ongoing supply partnerships.
