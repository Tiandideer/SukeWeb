Is the sheet suitable for outdoor use?
+
Many of our PMMA sheet products offer excellent weather resistance and UV stability, making them suitable for outdoor signage, light boxes, display systems and architectural applications.
Does the product come with protective film?
+
All sheets are supplied with protective film on both sides to prevent surface scratches during transportation and processing.
Can the sheets be further processed?
+
Yes. Most of our PMMA, PC and PS sheets support common fabrication processes, including cutting, drilling, engraving, thermoforming, bonding and CNC machining. Material recommendations can be provided based on your processing requirements.
What is the thickness tolerance?
+
Standard thickness tolerance is controlled within ±0.10 mm. Detailed technical data can be provided upon request.
Can the product replace tempered glass?
+
Yes. Our PMMA and PC sheets are often used as lightweight alternatives to tempered glass in display, lighting and signage applications. For structural or safety-critical installations, material suitability should be evaluated based on project requirements.
