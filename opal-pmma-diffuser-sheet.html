Opal PMMA Diffuser Sheet

About

Opal PMMA Diffuser Sheet is manufactured from high-quality PMMA resin using a stable extrusion process, providing a uniform and soft opal appearance with reliable light diffusion performance. It effectively conceals light sources and internal structures while delivering continuous and even illumination.

Multiple light transmission and haze levels are available to suit different light box structures, channel letters, and lighting product designs. The sheet also offers good fabrication performance and dimensional stability, making it suitable for cutting, engraving, thermoforming, and bonding.

Widely used in advertising light boxes, channel letters, and commercial signage systems, Opal PMMA Diffuser Sheet provides a reliable surface-light diffusion solution for commercial lighting and display applications.

Key Features

Excellent Light Source Concealment
Effectively hides LED light sources and internal structures for a clean illuminated appearance.
Uniform & Soft Illumination
Delivers smooth and even light distribution with reduced hotspots.
Consistent Opal Appearance
Maintains a uniform opal finish with minimal colour variation.
Customisable Diffusion Performance
Available in different light transmission and haze levels.
Easy Fabrication
Suitable for cutting, drilling, polishing, thermoforming, and bonding.
